# cellNexusWorkshop 0.1.0

* First BiocAsia workshop release.
* R workflow from a curated Human Cell Atlas query, through tidy exploration,
  sccomp composition, and donor-level pseudobulk expression.
* Respiratory ageing case study: immune composition, then epithelial gene expression.
* Cohort tissue comes from the CELLxGENE Census. The query article joins
  Census `tissue` in the open and derives the age columns there.
