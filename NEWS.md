# cellNexusWorkshop 0.1.0

* First BiocAsia workshop release.
* R workflow from a curated Human Cell Atlas query, through tidy exploration,
  sccomp composition, and donor-level pseudobulk expression.
* Case study and helper functions follow the respiratory ageing protocol at
  <https://github.com/stemangiola/book_chapter_Mangiola_et_al_cellNexus>.
